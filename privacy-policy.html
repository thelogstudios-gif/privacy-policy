Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026 | Last Updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Drop N Pop ("App", "Game", "we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when you use our App, as well as your privacy rights under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Developer Policies).

Summary: Drop N Pop is a casual free-to-play merge puzzle game. We do not sell your personal data. We use standard mobile industry SDKs (Google Play Services, AdMob, and Firebase) to display advertisements, analyze game performance, sync high scores/achievements, and fix crashes.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect directly identifiable personal information such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or government ID. When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically or via third-party service providers:

A. Device and Technical Information

  • Device Identifiers: IP address (anonymized/truncated), Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), Apple IDFA (if iOS), unique device identifiers, and hardware model.
  • Operating System & Environment: OS version, device language, screen resolution, time zone, and network carrier.

B. Usage and Game Telemetry Data

  • Gameplay Activity: Game sessions, scores achieved, high scores, levels reached, unlocked themes, achievement milestones, and in-game interactions.
  • Performance & Stability Data: App crashes, stack traces, system logs, memory statistics, and load times collected through Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring.

C. Google Play Games / Game Center (Optional)

  • If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the game accesses your public gamer tag/display name and avatar to display leaderboards, record achievements, and sync game saves.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Provide Game Functionality: Maintain your local save states, high scores, statistics, and unlocked rewards.
  • Advertising & Monetization: Serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Understand how players interact with game modes to balance difficulty and improve gameplay features.
  • Crash Diagnostics & Security: Monitor app health, fix bugs, and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Advertising and User Choices

You have control over interest-based advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and toggle on "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn off tracking permissions.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Retention and Security

We store game preferences and progress locally on your device storage (MMKV). We and our third-party SDK partners employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect data transmitted over network connections.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Global Privacy)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your data, including:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of your data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • The right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, you may contact us using the contact details below.
